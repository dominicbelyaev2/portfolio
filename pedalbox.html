Formula SAE Pedalbox

Role: pedal redesign, analysis & machining · Tools: FEA, topology optimization, manual + CNC machining · Timeline: 2025–26 season

Redesigned the brake and gas pedals for Northwestern's Formula SAE car using FEA and topology optimization, achieving roughly 30% mass reduction, then machined and assembled the parts in-house.

Approach

  • Topology optimization on the pedal faces and arms under braking/throttle load cases, then reinterpreted the result into a machinable geometry.
  • FEA verification of the final design against pedal-force requirements.
  • Machined and assembled the parts myself with the team's shop equipment.
